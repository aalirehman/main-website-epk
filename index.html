artist biography

Los Angeles-based producer and DJ Aali Rehman is drawn to deep, hypnotic, progressive sounds with dubby textures, atmospheric sound design, and stripped-back percussion.

His productions and sets focus on deep grooves, subtle movement, and immersive atmospheres made for late-night dancefloors and has shared the stage with Matador, Oliver Koletzki, Jonas Saalbach, Robag Wruhme, Theus Mago, Raxon, Henry Saiz, Nicole Moudaber, TDJ, Cristoph, Lindsey Herbert, Etari, Cassie Raptor, Mesme, Helsloot, Marie Nyx, Materielle, Wes Mills, Caravel, Sainte Vie, and more.
